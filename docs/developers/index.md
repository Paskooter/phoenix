# Build something that belongs on Jibo.

Build a skill, a touchscreen app or a theme. Start with working source, test on your computer, then submit your version for review.

**Who this is for:** anyone who wants to make something for Jibo, from a first touchscreen skill or theme to a published Skill Store listing. You need a computer; a Jibo of your own helps for testing.

## From an idea to the Skill Store

1. **Make it yours.** Start from an open [theme template](/developers/templates) and keep the original credits.
2. **Check your work.** Validate the manifest and images on your computer, then [try the pack on your own Jibo](/developers/test).
3. **Publish when ready.** Use your publisher identity, submit the pack for review, then choose when to list it. The [publishing guide](/developers/publishing) has every step.

## Build a skill

The [Skill SDK](/developers/skill-sdk) covers the supported data-only runtime, cards, buttons and speech. The [complete Hello Jibo example](/developers/skill-example) includes a downloadable project, computer preview and tests. Use the [API reference](/developers/skill-api) for exact manifest fields and publishing endpoints. Native JavaScript and local-test capabilities are a different contract, not part of declarative uploads.
