account's two Key_20160201.CreateRequest public keys with RSA/NONE/PKCS1Padding and delivered it
through Key_20160201.Share. The server accepts it verbatim - it never validates key length, and the
documented design says it must not (the crypto is the client's):

  doc 02fcdcc0facd9f46ba83d6a3: injected 31-byte fake key -> Share 200  ciphertext 256B
  doc 004a5173b692aed0d45b440f: injected 31-byte fake key -> Share 200  ciphertext 256B
